Design decisions

Show the state. Explain the next step.

Sources and destinations stay visible

Each display has its own source group. Room audio is separate, so selecting a different picture does not silently change the sound. Labels and selection states identify what the operator is changing.

Feedback follows the operation

The demo briefly shows a change in progress before confirming its simulated result. In a real installation, the interface must reflect the available device feedback and handle timeouts, rather than assume a command succeeded.

Faults have a visible boundary

The unavailable-screen scenario shows the affected device and disables its source choices. It illustrates how a partial fault can be communicated without making every room control unusable.

Important actions get confirmation

Shutdown has a confirmation step and a clear way back. Native buttons, keyboard focus and readable labels support browser use. Final touch targets, contrast and viewing distance are checked on the selected physical panel.

Who this is for

The honest answer about Starlink in Hamilton — depends which side of the escarpment you're on

Downtown Hamilton and the Mountain are not short on internet. Cable and fibre cover most of the built-up city, and if that is where you live, we are not going to talk you into a $449 Starlink install you do not need. The properties we actually go out to sit on the other side of that line — Flamborough, the escarpment edges around Ancaster and Dundas, and the farms strung out toward Binbrook, Mount Hope, Lynden and Caledonia, where the provider's coverage map is a lot more confident than the actual signal at the end of the driveway.

That rural belt is not small. Flamborough alone runs from Rockton and Freelton through Millgrove, Greensville and Copetown, and every one of those hamlets has streets where the nearest fibre node stops a concession or two short. Same story heading out past Glanbrook toward Binbrook and Caledonia — the new subdivision gets fibre, the farm next door gets a phone line from 1998 and a promise.

Three things come up constantly on these jobs. Escarpment terrain and mature trees. A lot of Ancaster and Dundas properties sit right on or under the Niagara Escarpment, and between the drop-off and the tree line a roof mount often cannot see enough clear sky — we survey first and move to a pole mount when it needs it. Steel-sided barns and shops. Steel kills Wi-Fi outright, so the fix is a proper Ethernet drop, not a stronger router. Older brick farmhouses, which get more common the further out you go — original masonry needs a cable entry point cut and sealed carefully, not just drilled through wherever is convenient.

Hamilton FAQ

Straight answers about Starlink in Hamilton

How much does Starlink installation cost in Hamilton?
$449 flat for a standard roof or gable mount, or $549 for a pole or non-penetrating mount. Hamilton and the surrounding area sit in our Zone B, so add a $59 travel fee. The number is confirmed in writing before we book, and it does not change at your door.
Do I actually need Starlink if I live in Hamilton?
If you're downtown or on the Mountain with working cable or fibre, no — Starlink is not an upgrade, and we'll tell you that outright. Out in Flamborough, on the escarpment around Ancaster and Dundas, or on the farms toward Binbrook, Mount Hope, Lynden and Caledonia, the answer is usually yes: the coverage map says serviced, the account rep says serviced, and the actual line stops well short of the house.
I'm on the escarpment with a lot of trees — does that rule Starlink out?
Not usually, but it changes the mount. A roof mount only works if the dish gets a clear view of open sky, and escarpment terrain plus mature trees often block that from the roofline. We check the sky view first and move to a pole or non-penetrating mount ($549) when the roof can't clear the tree line.
Can you get internet to a barn or shop?
Yes — Ethernet runs are $120 per drop, buried or run in conduit depending on the site, with outdoor-rated access points where you need actual Wi-Fi coverage inside. Steel siding kills a wireless signal outright, so a repeater in the farmhouse window will not reach a shop fifty metres away — a real drop will.
How soon can you come to Hamilton?
Weekend slots book first and usually go within the week. Hamilton is a straightforward Zone B run for us, and if you and a neighbour both need an install, ask us to pair the visit on one trip — that's when the $100 two-install discount applies.

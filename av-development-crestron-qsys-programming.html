Who this is for

AV integrators who need a programmer, and end users whose system never worked right

This page covers two different kinds of clients. The first is AV integrators and contractors who have a project that needs a Crestron or Q-SYS programmer and do not want to keep one on staff — send the drawings and the scope, get back a working, documented program. The second is end users: a corporate boardroom, a conference room or a home with AV hardware already on the wall that a previous integrator installed and either never finished programming or programmed badly. Both get the same person doing the work — a certified AV field engineer and control-system programmer who builds and programs commercial AV for a living.

That matters because most programming problems in a finished room are not a hardware problem. The gear is on the rack, the cable is run, and the touch panel still does not do what the room needs it to do. That is a programming job, not an install job, and it is what this page is for.

Why us

Programmed by someone who also installs the room

Field engineer, not just a programmer at a desk. The person who writes your Crestron or Q-SYS program is the same person who has pulled cable, mounted displays and commissioned rooms on site. Code gets written for the room that actually got built — the display that ended up on the wrong wall input, the DSP channel that got renamed halfway through the job — not just for the drawing it was supposed to match.

Documented and commented. Programs get delivered with comments that explain what the logic does and why, not a wall of unlabeled symbols. The next person who opens the file — you, your own tech, or another contractor — can follow it.

Project source handover. The proposal defines the source files, documentation and licence rights provided to you and whoever services the system after us. Third-party licensing still applies.

How we work

Remote-first, fixed-price, one person accountable

Remote-first. Most of the programming and diagnostic work happens over the network, which is why we take clients across Canada and the US this way. On-site visits for pull, terminate and hands-on commissioning are available across the GTA and Ontario.

Scope call. We start with a call to go over the equipment list, the drawings if you have them, and what the room or the project actually needs to do.

Fixed-price proposal. After the scope call you get a written, fixed-price proposal — not an hourly estimate that can drift once work starts.

Development. Programming happens against the agreed scope, with progress checked against real hardware or a test environment wherever possible, not just simulated on a laptop.

Factory or on-site commissioning. Programs get tested and commissioned — remotely against the client's or integrator's hardware, in an agreed test environment, or on-site for GTA/Ontario jobs — before handoff.

Handover with docs. You get the source files, program documentation, and a walkthrough of what was built, so the system does not depend on us being reachable to keep running.

AV development FAQ

Straight answers about Crestron & Q-SYS programming

Can you take over a system another integrator built?
We can assess an existing system with the owner's authorization. Editing depends on available source files, credentials and compatible tools. Network access alone does not guarantee source recovery. A replacement program is scoped separately.
Do we get the source code?
Project-specific source files and documentation are included as agreed in the written proposal. Third-party libraries and separately licensed commercial modules remain subject to their own licence terms.
Do you work as a subcontractor for integrators?
Yes. We can work from an integrator's drawings and agreed scope. Responsibilities, access, confidentiality and handover requirements are confirmed before work starts.
Can you work remotely?
Remote development and diagnostics depend on authorized access, source files and compatible tools. Physical verification may require an on-site technician. GTA and Ontario site visits are scoped separately.
What if my device has no Crestron module?
Send the device model, protocol documentation and required controls. We can assess a custom driver or module, including how it will be tested with the target hardware. Scope and deliverables are confirmed before development starts.
Do you do Q-SYS as well as Crestron?
Yes. Q-SYS Designer, Lua scripting and UCI design get the same attention as Crestron SIMPL, SIMPL#, SIMPL#Pro and 4-Series/VC-4 programming, plus Biamp Tesira TTP and Dante/AES67 routing where a room needs it. Tell us the platform and the scope and we will quote it the same way.
Are the Wirewolf commercial modules available yet?
Contact us for current module availability and licensing. This page does not offer a downloadable module or promise a release date.

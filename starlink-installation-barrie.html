Who this is for

The county side of Simcoe, where the map says “serviced” and the driveway disagrees

Barrie itself is well served. Drive ten minutes in any direction and that stops being true. The properties we install Starlink at are in Oro-Medonte, Springwater, Essa, Adjala–Tosorontio, the concessions off Highway 11, 26 and 27, and the stretch between Innisfil and Cookstown where every provider’s coverage map claims service that nobody can actually buy.

If you are inside Barrie city limits with working cable, we will tell you plainly that Starlink is not an upgrade for you. We would rather lose the job than sell you a $449 install you did not need. What we do want are the addresses where the real options are a fixed-wireless tower you cannot see, an LTE stick with a data cap, or nothing at all.

Rural Simcoe brings its own install problems, and they are not the ones a downtown crew is ready for. Outbuildings. A house router does not reach a steel-sided shop or a barn a hundred metres out — steel and concrete stop Wi-Fi cold, and a repeater in the kitchen window does nothing. That needs a real Ethernet drop, $120 each, buried or in conduit. Wind exposure. Open farmland means the mount has to be rated for it, not just screwed to a soffit. Snow. A dish you cannot reach in February is a dish that will be offline in February, which is why we often recommend a pole mount out here instead of putting it on the roof.

Barrie FAQ

Straight answers about Starlink in Simcoe County

How much does Starlink installation cost in Barrie?
$449 flat for a standard roof or gable mount, or $549 for a pole or non-penetrating mount. Barrie and Simcoe County sit in our Zone B, so add a $59 travel fee. The number is confirmed in writing before we book, and it does not change at your door.
I live in Barrie and already have cable. Why would I want Starlink?
In the built-up parts of Barrie you probably would not. The properties we install at are the ones on the county side — Oro-Medonte, Springwater, Essa, the concessions off Highway 11 and 27 — where the cable ends a kilometre before the driveway does. If you are inside city limits with good service, we will tell you honestly that Starlink is not an upgrade.
Can you get internet to a barn, shop or second building?
Yes, and it is most of what we do out here. Ethernet runs are $120 per drop, buried or run in conduit as the site needs, with outdoor-rated access points for coverage in a shop or barn. Steel siding and concrete both kill Wi-Fi, so a repeater in the house will not do it — a real drop will.
Can you mount the dish on a pole instead of my roof?
Yes — $549 for a pole or non-penetrating mount. On rural properties this is often the better answer: it keeps the roof intact, gets the dish clear of the tree line, and puts it somewhere you can actually reach to clear snow.
How soon can you come to Barrie?
Weekend slots book first and usually go within the week. Barrie is a short run for us at Zone B, so we can often pair two bookings in the same area on one day — if a neighbour needs an install too, ask us to pair them and split the travel fee.

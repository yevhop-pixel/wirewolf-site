SEC.07 — FAQ

Straight answers

Do you have insurance my condo board will accept?
Yes — we carry commercial general liability insurance and can send a certificate to your property manager before the visit. Just ask when booking.
Do I order the Starlink kit myself?
Yes — order the kit at starlink.com (it ships in a few days), then book us. We handle everything from the box onward: mount, routing, weatherproofing, Wi-Fi coverage. Not sure which kit? Text us first and we’ll tell you exactly what to order.
What if my wall is brick / concrete / stone?
Same price. We carry the anchors and bits for every wall type — drywall with steel studs, brick, poured concrete, stone veneer, log walls at the cottage.
What's the warranty?
Lifetime workmanship warranty on every mount we install: if anything we bolted to your wall ever loosens, we come back and fix it free. Equipment carries its manufacturer warranty.
How fast can you come?
Weekend slots book first — typically we can be at your place the same week, and often same-day for Zone A TV mounting. Text us and we’ll give you the next real slot, not a call-centre promise.
Why “Wirewolf”?
Because we hunt in a pack, we travel far, and we never leave a wire showing. Also, you won’t forget it — which is the point.
